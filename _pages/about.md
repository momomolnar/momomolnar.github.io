<p class="page__intro">I am a Research Scientist at Southwest Research Institute in Boulder, Colorado, where I study how the Sun's magnetic field shapes its outer atmosphere &mdash; using spectropolarimetry, space- and ground-based spectroscopy, and physics-informed machine learning.</p>

Before joining SwRI in October 2024, I was an Advanced Study Program Postdoctoral Fellow at NSF NCAR's High Altitude Observatory (2022&ndash;2024), where I worked on Hanle-effect magnetic diagnostics and neural-field inversion tools, and led the CHEESE experiment during the April 8, 2024 total solar eclipse. I completed my PhD in Astrophysical and Planetary Sciences at the University of Colorado Boulder in 2022, after an MSc at CU Boulder and a BSc in Physics from MIT.

## What I work on

<div class="focus-cards">

<div class="focus-card">
  <h3 class="focus-card__title">Coronal magnetometry</h3>
  <p>Measuring the coronal magnetic field through the unsaturated Hanle effect in the He&nbsp;I 1083&nbsp;nm line, with DKIST/Cryo-NIRSP, UCoMP, and dedicated eclipse experiments.</p>
</div>

<div class="focus-card">
  <h3 class="focus-card__title">The transition region with SPICE</h3>
  <p>Solar Orbiter/SPICE diagnostics: Doppler velocities, FIP-bias and elemental abundance evolution during flares, and coordinated observations with EUI-HRI.</p>
</div>

<div class="focus-card">
  <h3 class="focus-card__title">Physics-informed machine learning</h3>
  <p>PINN-ME and related neural-field methods for fast, accurate Milne-Eddington spectropolarimetric inversions, and for radiative transfer computations.</p>
</div>

<div class="focus-card">
  <h3 class="focus-card__title">Waves and atmospheric heating</h3>
  <p>Acoustic and Alfv&eacute;nic wave energy from the chromosphere to the corona, combining ALMA, IBIS, IRIS, and DKIST observations with 3D rMHD models.</p>
</div>

</div>
