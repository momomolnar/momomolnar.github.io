I am a Research Scientist at Southwest Research Institute in Boulder, Colorado. My work focuses on solar transition-region and coronal diagnostics, spectropolarimetry, and physics-informed machine learning methods for solar magnetic field inference.

Before joining SwRI (October 2024-present), I was an Advanced Study Program Postdoctoral Fellow at NCAR/HAO (October 2022-October 2024), where I worked on Hanle-effect magnetic diagnostics and neural-field inversion tools, and led the CHEESE eclipse experiment during the April 8, 2024 total solar eclipse.

I completed my PhD in Astrophysical and Planetary Sciences at the University of Colorado Boulder (awarded August 2022), after an MSc at CU Boulder and a BSc in Physics from MIT.

This website includes publications, talks, and a full CV. For the latest complete CV, see the PDF linked on the [CV page](/cv/).
