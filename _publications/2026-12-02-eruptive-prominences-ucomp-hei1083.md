summary: >-
  Coronal mass ejections often carry cooler prominence material tangled in their magnetic
  structure, and the neutral atoms in that material emit brightly in permitted lines, which raises
  the prospect of measuring the magnetic field inside an eruption. This paper presents
  observations from the Upgraded Coronal Multi-channel Polarimeter that clearly show neutral
  helium at 1083 nm in erupting prominences as they move through the lower and middle corona. The
  emission stays detectable out to the edge of the instrument's field of view, near two solar
  radii, complementing existing extreme ultraviolet and white-light coronal imaging. Because the
  line is spectrally resolved, the observations also yield line-of-sight velocities, which could
  allow earlier and more accurate identification of Earth-directed eruptions for space weather
  forecasting.
