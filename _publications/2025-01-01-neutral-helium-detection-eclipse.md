summary: >-
  Earlier eclipse measurements found strong He I 1083 nm emission above the lunar limb, which
  would imply a surprising quantity of neutral helium surviving at coronal temperatures. This
  paper tests that interpretation with CHEESE, a purpose-built spectrograph operated during the
  April 8, 2024 total solar eclipse. The observations detect He I 1083 nm, the forbidden coronal
  Fe XIII 1074.7 nm line, and the chromospheric Paschen-gamma line of hydrogen at 1093.8 nm. The
  decisive detail is that the helium and Paschen-gamma signals appear not only in the corona but
  also on the lunar disc itself, which points to a non-solar origin for the helium signal and
  argues against the presence of abundant neutral helium in the corona.
