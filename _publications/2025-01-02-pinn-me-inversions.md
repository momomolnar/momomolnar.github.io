summary: >-
  PINN ME recasts Milne-Eddington spectropolarimetric inversion as a physics-informed neural
  network. Rather than fitting each pixel on its own, the network represents the parameter space
  itself, mapping time and position coordinates to Milne-Eddington parameters, synthesizing the
  corresponding Stokes profiles, and iteratively minimizing the mismatch with the observed ones.
  The instrument point spread function is built directly into the forward model. Tested against a
  predefined parameter space and synthetic profiles from a radiative magnetohydrodynamic
  simulation, the method develops an intrinsic coupling across space and time that suppresses much
  of the observational noise while keeping memory use modest even for extended fields of view.
  Applied to real observations, it yields high spatial coherence and resolves small-scale
  structure in both strong-field and weak-field regions.
