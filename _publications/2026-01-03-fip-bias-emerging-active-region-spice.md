summary: >-
  How does elemental composition evolve across an emerging active region, and does the pattern
  follow the magnetic topology? Using the SPICE instrument on Solar Orbiter, this study measures
  extreme ultraviolet lines from ions formed between the upper chromosphere and the low corona,
  then builds relative abundance maps through differential emission measure analysis. Fan loops,
  loop footpoints and active region boundaries show coronal composition, while the core of the
  active region stays closer to photospheric. The magnesium to neon ratio rises slowly and
  steadily, beginning near 1.5 and increasing by about half over two days. The measurements agree
  well with the ponderomotive force model, supporting fractionation driven by Alfven waves, and
  the sulfur to oxygen behaviour points to resonant waves fractionating plasma in transition
  region structures.
