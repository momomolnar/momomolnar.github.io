summary: >-
  A systematic map of how binaries containing a white dwarf accretor evolve. The study follows a
  grid of 56,000 starting models through fully non-conservative Roche-lobe overflow with the MESA
  stellar evolution code, spanning fourteen white dwarf masses, forty-three donor masses and a
  hundred orbital periods. Tracks in the orbital period and donor mass plane show where classical
  cataclysmic variables, AM CVn binaries, supersoft X-ray sources and systems with giant donors
  spend their time, along with accretion rates and donor compositions. The work quantifies how the
  minimum orbital period depends on the donor's chemistry, updates the relation between orbital
  period and white dwarf mass for systems whose progenitors lost their envelopes through stable
  mass transfer, and marks where accretion disks stay stable against the thermal-viscous
  instability and where LISA might detect a gravitational wave signal.
