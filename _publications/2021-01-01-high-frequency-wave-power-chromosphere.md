summary: >-
  How much energy do high-frequency acoustic waves actually deliver to the chromosphere? This
  study measures power spectra between 5 and 50 mHz in the Ca II 854.2 nm and hydrogen alpha lines
  and in the millimeter continuum at 1.25 and 3 mm, combining the Dunn Solar Telescope with ALMA.
  Almost every diagnostic follows a power law whose slope, peak and noise floor depend on whether
  the region is internetwork, network or plage. Two fields of view, one near disk center and one
  near the limb, help separate vertical from transverse motions. Comparing the observations with
  synthetic observables from the time-dependent radiative hydrodynamic RADYN code puts the
  acoustic flux in the middle chromosphere at up to about 1 kW per square meter, which falls short
  of what is needed to maintain the quiet chromosphere, contrary to several earlier reports.
