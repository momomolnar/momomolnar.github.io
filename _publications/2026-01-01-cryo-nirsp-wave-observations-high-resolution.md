summary: >-
  A survey of high-frequency wave behaviour in closed active-region structures, using the Cryo-
  NIRSP infrared slit spectropolarimeter at DKIST to observe the corona off the limb in the
  forbidden Fe XIII 1074 and 1079 nm lines. The aperture of DKIST allows a cadence faster than one
  second at a spatial resolution of two to five arcseconds, and the power spectra show enhanced
  wave power extending out to 100 mHz. The data also reveal a statistically significant anti-
  correlation between the peak intensity and the width of the Fe XIII 1074 nm line, which may
  indicate the presence of compressive magnetohydrodynamic modes. Together these results show what
  DKIST's spectropolarimetric capability adds to the study of coronal waves.
