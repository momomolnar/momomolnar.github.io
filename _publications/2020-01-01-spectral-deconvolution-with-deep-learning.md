summary: >-
  Instruments that sample a spectral line through a broad or multi-peaked transmission profile
  blur the profile they record. This paper trains convolutional neural networks to undo that
  blurring, recovering the original line profiles from the FISS spectrograph at Big Bear and from
  IBIS at the Dunn Solar Telescope. Across granulation, plage and pores the method holds
  photometric precision better than one percent, and it is fast enough for production data
  volumes, running about a hundred thousand deconvolutions per second on a CPU and ten times that
  on a GPU. The authors test it across several IBIS spectral resolution modes and on a synthetic
  multi-peaked transmission profile of the kind planned for the Visible Tunable Filter at DKIST,
  and use a dimensionality analysis to show where the approach reaches its limits.
