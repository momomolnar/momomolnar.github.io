summary: >-
  Force-free extrapolations rest on an assumption that fails in the dense lower atmosphere, which
  limits how faithfully they reconstruct the coronal field from photospheric magnetograms. This
  paper feeds chromospheric field measurements into physics-informed neural networks, adding a
  flexible height-mapping model that accounts for the different formation heights of the various
  magnetograms. Tests against analytical and simulated fields show that the extra chromospheric
  constraint improves the reconstruction significantly. Applied to chromospheric line-of-sight
  magnetograms from SOLIS/VSM together with photospheric vector magnetograms from SDO/HMI, the
  results agree better with coronal structures seen in extreme ultraviolet images. The method also
  returns an estimate of the corrugation of the observed magnetogram surface as a by-product.
