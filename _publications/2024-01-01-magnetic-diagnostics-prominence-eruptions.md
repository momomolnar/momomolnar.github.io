summary: >-
  The direction of the magnetic field inside an Earth-directed eruption is the missing ingredient
  for forecasting its geomagnetic effect, and it is not something current coronal observations
  routinely provide. This paper shows that the unsaturated Hanle effect in the He I 1083 nm line
  can supply it. Modeling an erupting prominence as a toroidal helical flux rope embedded in a
  hydrostatically stratified corona, the authors show that the geometry of the line's linear
  polarization distinguishes different field orientations under broad and practical viewing
  conditions, which would constrain the north-south field component arriving at 1 AU. Observations
  from the Sun-Earth Lagrange points emerge as especially favourable vantage points for a synoptic
  instrument, and a signal-to-noise calculation indicates that a space-borne coronagraph with an
  aperture of only about five centimeters would be sufficient.
